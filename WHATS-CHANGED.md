# What's changed

This page lists the changes to Manhattan Community Mapping, the map survey BetaNYC built for the 2026 Manhattan Borough President convention, that a person filling it in would notice: new questions, changed wording, and fixes. Newest first. The survey's answers are collected privately and are not published on this site.

- **History begins:** 2026-01-30, the day the survey first went online. Every change since then is listed.

To report a bug in this survey or ask for a new feature, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbp-mapping-manhattan/issues).

## 2026-11-12: A What's changed page

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** A new page lists every change to the survey a reader would notice, newest first. The survey page links to it at the bottom.
- **More detail:** [What's changed on GitHub](WHATS-CHANGED.md)

## 2026-02-20: Questions match the printed maps

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** The wording of the questions now matches the printed maps used at the convention, and the kinds of map were renamed to match, for example "Parks & Open Space" instead of "Parks". The choices for what a pin marks are now buttons to pick one, instead of a drop-down list.
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbp-mapping-manhattan/commit/7420247)

## 2026-02-19: Housing added as a kind of map

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** Housing is now one of the kinds of map, with two choices for a pin: "More new housing" and "Affordable housing preservation".
- **More detail:** [First commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbp-mapping-manhattan/commit/5bba711) · [Second commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbp-mapping-manhattan/commit/29709d0)

## 2026-02-18: New survey wording

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** The text of the survey was rewritten.
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbp-mapping-manhattan/commit/a20f1f5)
